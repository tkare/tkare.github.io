About

I graduated from the University of Maryland, College Park with a B.S. in Computer Science and a minor in Philosophy.

I pick things up, dive deep, and keep only what survives. It's a pattern that goes back to cheap robot kits and books I wasn't old enough to understand: a search for ideas that hold up under pressure, builds that last through real use, and visuals that earn their space.

Computer science taught me to build; philosophy taught me to articulate; both gave me ways to think creatively. After hours, it's the same itch in different forms: thrifting and sewing, museum trips, and working out what dessert flavor combination to try next (ask me about my Coca-Cola peanut butter cookies).
